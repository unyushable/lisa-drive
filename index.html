Lisa Drive

Lisa Drive is a private tool used by one person. It connects that person's own voice assistant, running on their own computer, to their own Google account.

What it does:

  • Google Drive: lists, searches, downloads and uploads the owner's own files, and creates a share link when the owner asks for one. It never deletes files.
  • Gmail: reads mail from banks and billers, read-only, to track dues and payments. It never sends, changes or deletes mail.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
