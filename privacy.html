Lisa Drive: privacy policy

Lisa Drive is a private tool with a single user, who is also its owner and developer.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent it accesses the owner's own Google Drive files and reads the owner's own Gmail messages (read-only).

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own files and to track their own bills and payments. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Where the data is kept

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no server run by this app. Nothing is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local data at any time, and can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Use the support email address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
